BREAD RUN

Rob the block. Feed the family. Watch the money climb.

Walk into a shop, take things off the shelves while the clerk looks away, or pull FIRE to threaten the clerk for the register, then get out the door and carry it home before the cupboard runs empty. Trip the alarm and the police come running. The counter never stops, and it runs fastest when you are being chased.

Also: the streets have zombies (their leader is El Jefe), bears called Gemma, and a giant otter called Julia Otter who only eats zombies. Any car can be taken and driven through them. On Medium and Hard you start with a pistol and buy the slingshot and bazooka from gold crates around town. Days turn to night, and it rains or snows every other day. Walk south off the last street to reach the beach: nothing follows you there and you heal. You have a pistol, a bazooka that levels buildings, and a slingshot that lobs grenades over rooftops.

Left thumb moves. DASH is a short burst. On a keyboard: arrows or WASD, Space to dash or grab, F to fire or threaten, Q to switch weapon, E to take or leave a car, M to mug someone with their hands up.
